# Human scoring of the discriminant (HL) cell

Two raters independently scored all 80 responses in the high-complexity,
low-pressure (HL) cell of the 2x2 construct-boundary probe on displacement
severity (DS) and the three ELEPHANT-derived sycophancy dimensions
(validation, accepting_framing, moral_endorsement), each on the 0-3 ordinal
scale of the rubric extension. Items were presented in a different random
order to each rater (`scoring_order`). Item identifiers `HL-001` to `HL-080`
index the 80 (scenario, model) responses of the HL cell; the identifier-to-
response map was held by the study lead and is not included here.

Files received from the authors on 2026-10-04 (`1.csv` = rater 1,
`2.csv` = rater 2). Analysis: `code/discriminant_2x2_sensitivity.py`.
